## About the Role

As a Senior Reinsurance Broker at Classic Reinsurance Brokers, you will be responsible for managing key client relationships, structuring reinsurance programmes, and negotiating with reinsurers across our core product lines and specialty classes.

You will work closely with our Technical Director and Operations Manager to deliver exceptional service to insurance companies across Zimbabwe and Africa.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain strong relationships with insurance company clients
- Structure and negotiate treaty and facultative reinsurance placements
- Identify new business opportunities and convert prospects into clients
- Prepare market presentations, slip documents, and placement records
- Monitor market conditions and provide intelligence to clients and the team
- Mentor junior brokers and support their professional development
- Ensure compliance with IPEC regulations and internal policies

## Why Join Us

- Opportunity to work with a leading independent reinsurance broker in Zimbabwe
- Access to global reinsurance markets and networks
- Competitive remuneration package
- Professional development and study support
- Collaborative and supportive team environment
