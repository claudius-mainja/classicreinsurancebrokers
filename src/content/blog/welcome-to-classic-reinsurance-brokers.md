Welcome to the official insights page of Classic Reinsurance Brokers, Zimbabwe's premier independent reinsurance intermediary. Through this platform, we will share market intelligence, industry analysis, and thought leadership on reinsurance and risk management in Zimbabwe and across Africa.

## Why Reinsurance Matters

Reinsurance is the backbone of the insurance industry. It provides insurance companies with the capacity to underwrite larger risks, protects against catastrophic losses, and stabilises underwriting results over time. For a growing insurance market like Zimbabwe, access to reliable reinsurance capacity is essential for sustainable growth.

## Our Commitment to Zimbabwe's Insurance Industry

Since our founding in 2015, Classic Reinsurance Brokers has been dedicated to serving insurance companies across Zimbabwe with professional, independent reinsurance broking services. We combine local market intelligence with extensive relationships in global and regional reinsurance markets to deliver optimal outcomes for our clients.

### What Sets Us Apart

- **Specialist Focus**: We are purely a reinsurance broker — not a composite broker juggling direct insurance and reinsurance. This singular focus means deeper expertise and better outcomes for our clients.

- **Market Access**: Through our relationships with leading reinsurers including Lloyd's, London company market, African reinsurers, and international markets, we provide our clients with access to the capacity and expertise they need.

- **Client-First Approach**: Every client relationship is built on trust, transparency, and a deep understanding of their unique portfolio needs.
